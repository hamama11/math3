Step 02. 전개 및 소거 (Difference Expansion)

$$ An^2 + Bn + C - \{A(n^2-2n+1) + Bn - B + C\} $$

Step 03. 일반항 정리 (Resulting Arithmetic Sequence)

$$ = 2An + (B-A) $$

The Core Formula
